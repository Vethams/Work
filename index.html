✏️ Quick Resume Improvements
  • 1
    Add a GitHub Profile Link

    Upload your two projects (Attendance System + Charity System) to GitHub with a README. Paste the GitHub URL next to your portfolio link. Recruiters and developers check this first.

  • 2
    Quantify Your Achievements

    Instead of "Developed a web-based attendance system," write "Developed a web-based attendance system managing records for 200+ students, reducing manual tracking time by ~40%." Numbers get noticed.

  • 3
    Fix the Aggregate Formatting

    Your resume says "9.34%" but you mean 9.34 CGPA. Clarify: "CGPA: 9.34 / 10." Recruiters may misread the percentage sign as a percentage score (which would be extremely low).

  • 4
    Add a "Key Projects" Tech Stack Badge Row

    Below each project, add: Technologies: HTML5 | CSS3 | PHP | MySQL | JavaScript | Bootstrap. This ensures ATS systems pick up all your skills even if the recruiter skims quickly.

  • 5
    Mention BSNL Internship Duration

    Add the exact dates (Month Year – Month Year) and mention it gave you a BSNL Certificate. Duration + certification signals commitment and official recognition.

  • 6
    Add a "Languages Known" Row

    Tamil (Native), English (CEFR B1 – Cambridge Certified). Many Tamil Nadu employers and BPOs specifically want Tamil + English bilingual candidates. Make it visible.

  • 7
    Create a 1-Page ATS-Friendly Version

    Keep the full resume as backup, but prepare a clean 1-page PDF (no tables, no columns) for uploading to Naukri, LinkedIn, and Indeed. ATS systems often fail to parse multi-column formats.
